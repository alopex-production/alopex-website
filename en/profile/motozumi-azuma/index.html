DIRECTOR’S VISION

The emotions before words.

A passing remark, the weight of a silence, a glance held for a moment longer. Cinema can reveal the smallest movements of feeling hidden within everyday life.

Turning everyday emotions into cinema.

A background in music informs Azuma’s sensitivity to rhythm and the passage of time in moving images.

02 /

THE JOURNEY

Born on March 3, 1990 in Akashi, Hyogo, Motozumi Azuma is a film director, screenwriter and acting instructor based in Harima, Hyogo. He leads ALOPEX production, working across filmmaking, video production and actor development.

1990Born in Akashi, Hyogo. Developed an early interest in music.
2010Graduated from Osaka School of Music.
2014Made a nationwide debut as guitarist and songwriter in a three-piece rock band.
2018Opened and operated a restaurant in Himeji, Hyogo.
LATERShifted his creative focus to filmmaking, working across writing, directing, cinematography and editing.
TODAYLeads ALOPEX production, creating films and videos while supporting actor development.
